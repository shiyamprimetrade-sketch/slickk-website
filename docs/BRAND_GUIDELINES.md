# SLICKK brand guidelines

The original SLICKK identity uses a constructed angular S. Six geometric letter outlines form the wordmark. It does not depend on a third-party display font. The S also serves as the compact symbol, avatar and favicon. The mark conveys a precise cut between design and engineering; it is not a claim about client results.

## Files and regeneration

`node scripts/prepare-brand-assets.mjs` generates all masters and exports from one source definition:

- `public/brand/slickk-wordmark-{dark,light,blue}.{svg,png,jpg}`
- `public/brand/slickk-symbol-{dark,light,blue}.{svg,png,jpg}`
- `public/brand/slickk-horizontal-{dark,light,blue}.{svg,png,jpg}`
- `public/brand/slickk-avatar.{svg,png,jpg}`
- `public/brand/slickk-social.svg`, `public/images/agency-social.jpg`
- `public/favicon.svg`, `public/favicon-{96,192,512}.png`, `public/apple-touch-icon.png`

SVG is the editable master; PNG retains transparency; JPEG uses a solid ivory or obsidian background. The hero and site header use SVG. The social image is 1200 × 630; the avatar is square. The SLICKK mark is original site work, while Inter Variable is locally hosted under the included SIL Open Font License.

## Use

Use the light wordmark on obsidian, dark on ivory, and blue as an accent. Keep at least the height of one letter stroke of empty space around the mark. Do not distort, recolor with unapproved gradients, or place the dark mark on dark material. The compact symbol needs an accessible text name when used as a link. The tagline is **We Design. We Build. We Elevate.**; use the full business name **Slickk Digital Solutions** in business and legal contexts.

Minimum recommended display sizes: wordmark 120 CSS px wide, symbol 32 CSS px square. Check contrast and small-scale legibility in the actual use context. This is a usage guideline, not a registered trademark or legal rights opinion.

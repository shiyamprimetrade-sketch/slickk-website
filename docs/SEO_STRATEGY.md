## Content and internal links

Each indexable page should have a distinct title, useful description, one descriptive primary heading and a canonical URL. Guides answer preparation questions and link to relevant inquiry choices; service pages link to pricing, honest concept examples and useful guides. Descriptive links should help visitors navigate even without keyword research. Avoid unsupported superlatives, fabricated reviews and promised search positions.

Keep sitemap entries limited to canonical public pages. Private inboxes, operational endpoints and error pages are not acquisition content. Robots exclusions are not authentication. Check redirects, canonical URLs, sitemap status and broken internal links after deployment; this document does not certify their final production state.

## Favicon and site name

Official guidance verified on 28 October 2026:

- Google requires a square favicon at least 8×8 pixels and recommends a size larger than 48×48. Use a stable, crawlable branded asset; allow access to the homepage and favicon. Google treats favicons by hostname. Implementation makes a site eligible but does not guarantee display. [Google favicon documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/favicon-in-search)
- Set the preferred site name in the homepage’s existing `WebSite` structured-data node with `name` and canonical homepage `url`; an honest `alternateName` can help. Keep visible branding and `og:site_name` consistent. Google’s site-name selection is automated and supported at domain/subdomain level, not per subdirectory. [Google site-name documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/site-names)

Use SLICKK consistently as the short brand and Slickk Digital Solutions as the full identity. Validate markup syntax and actual rendered source. Do not create duplicate WebSite nodes solely to repeat a preference.

## Owner verification and measurement

Google Search Console and Bing ownership verification, sitemap submission and indexing inspection require the appropriate owner account; they are not established by this document. After access is available, inspect the homepage and representative service/guide URLs, submit the canonical sitemap, and record actual indexing status. Reprocessing timing and search presentation are controlled by the search engines.

Review impressions, queries, clicks and qualified inquiries only after sufficient real observations exist. Consent-based interaction counts are useful directional signals but do not establish conversion attribution or revenue. Track qualified inquiries separately with minimal personal information. Use observed questions to revise content; do not manufacture numerical targets or testimonials to fill evidence gaps.

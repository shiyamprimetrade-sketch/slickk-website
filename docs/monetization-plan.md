# Monetization plan — 29 October 2026

SLICKK sells scoped services. Existing published owner-approved starting prices: logo BDT 2,000, brand identity BDT 8,000, brochure BDT 5,000, business website BDT 10,000, social creative BDT 5,000/month. Other work needs a written quote. No payment service, subscription, customer accounts, or usage-billed API exists.

| Model | Customer value | Build / operating cost | Gross margin and funnel | Privacy and decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Project inquiries **chosen** | Clear scope and direct consultation | Low: existing pricing, quote form, private D1 inbox; hosting and human sales time | `(collected project fee - delivery and processing cost) / collected fee`; organic visit → inquiry → qualified lead → signed scope → paid invoice | Consent, retention, access control; continue existing flow |
| Business plans / retainer | Repeat creative or SEO delivery | Medium: capacity, contract, monthly reporting | Price and hours unknown; quote individually, no automatic recurring billing | Owner must approve scope and price |
| Freemium subscription | No current self-serve product | High build/support cost | Cannot estimate a credible product margin or signup funnel | Reject for current site |
| Premium features | No current user accounts/features | High | No price or activation evidence | Defer |
| Usage-based API billing | No public API | High metering, abuse and payment cost | No measurable unit economics | Defer |
| Credits | No consumable product | High accounting/support cost | No credible credit value | Defer |
| Affiliates | Potential tool recommendations, but no vetted products | Medium editorial/compliance cost | Commission terms unknown | No endorsements or links until verified and approved |
| Advertising | Little demonstrated traffic; harms service conversion | Medium policy/UX cost | RPM unknown | Defer until real traffic and owner approval |

The implemented foundation is contextual links from three guides to paid services and reciprocal links from matching detailed service pages. Existing CTA and consent-based event counting remain unchanged. This is a navigation improvement, not a claim of measured conversion lift. No payments, ads, or third-party trackers were activated.

Operating rule: count qualified inquiries in the private inbox; record won work and collected revenue in an owner-controlled ledger outside public analytics. Do not put lead names, email, or deal value into browser events. Pricing and margin must be reviewed with actual delivery hours and costs before financial targets are set.

- `public/images/portfolio/*.svg`: five original code-native concept illustrations generated by `scripts/prepare-v4-assets.mjs`. NORTH, ATELIER, FOCUS and STILL are fictional demonstration identities. Dashboard values are sample data. These assets do not document client work or commercial outcomes.
- `public/images/agency-social.jpg`: 1200 × 630 JPEG generated locally from the existing original `public/agency-social.svg` with the installed Sharp package.
- Portfolio images are responsive, explicitly sized and lazy-loaded below the fold. No stock photographs or third-party client logos were added.
- Rebuild these agency assets with `node scripts/prepare-v4-assets.mjs`.
